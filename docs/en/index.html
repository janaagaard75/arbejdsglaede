Happiness at work

Privacy policy Dansk

Happiness at work is the companion app to the board game about happiness at work, played at Henrik Leth's workshops for workplaces. The app keeps track of your happiness score as you scan the QR codes revealed during the game.

Get the app

Scan the QR code with your phone to download Happiness at work:

Support

The camera does not start

The app needs access to the camera to scan the QR codes. If you declined access, you can grant it again under Settings → Happiness at work → Camera on iPhone, or under Settings → Apps → Happiness at work → Permissions on Android.

The QR code is not recognized

Make sure there is good light and that the whole QR code is visible to the camera. The app only recognizes the QR codes from the game and shows "Unknown QR code" for any other code.

Starting over

Tap Reset and confirm to set the score back to the start and remove all smileys and hearts.

Where is the score stored?

The score is only stored on your phone, so it is still there when you open the app again. It is removed if you delete the app.
