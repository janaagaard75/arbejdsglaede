Privacy policy

Happiness at work Dansk

The app Happiness at work is published by Henrik Leth. It requires no account, and it collects no information about who you are.

The camera

The camera is only used to scan the QR codes from the game. The images are processed on the phone and are neither stored nor sent anywhere.

The score

The score is only stored on your phone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Updates and troubleshooting

The app downloads updates from Expo, which also builds it. For that purpose, and so we can discover errors and slow screens, the app sends technical information to Expo: the app version, operating system, phone model, language, error reports, which screens are opened and how long they take to open. The information is tied to a random ID for the installation and not to you.

What we do not do

The app shows no ads, does not track you across apps and websites, and does not share data with anyone else.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, write to henrikleth@henrikleth.com.
